About this planner

Built by Daniel Llobet (School for Advanced Studies, MDC Wolfson) as a student project. Questions about the scorecard itself go to iCED at iCED@mdc.edu.

If you sign in, your progress is stored in a Firebase project owned by Daniel Llobet on Google Cloud. Only you can read it. Delete it any time from the account menu. This is not an MDC or iCED system.

This planner is an unofficial planning tool, not an MDC or iCED system, and it does not submit anything on your behalf. Progress is stored only in your browser until you export it.
