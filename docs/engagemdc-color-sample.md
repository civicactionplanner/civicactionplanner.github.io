# EngageMDC color sample

Sampled September 30, 2026 from https://engage.mdc.edu (which resolves to
https://mdc.givepulse.com/group/676261-EngageMDC) at 1280px, computed styles only.
No colors or images were lifted from the page assets; this records what the
platform paints so the planner's palette can rhyme with it.

| What | Computed value |
| --- | --- |
| Page ground and header | rgb(255, 255, 255), dark text |
| Body text | rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.87) |
| Primary action buttons (Add Impact) | bright blue, about #056CF2 |

Non-neutral colors by painted area:

1. rgb(34, 70, 142) (#22468E), about 462,000 px2: the banner band and collage tiles
2. rgb(250, 235, 190) (#FAEBBE), about 40,000 px2: the warm notice box ("Hello MDC Sharks")
3. rgb(5, 108, 242) (#056CF2): action buttons and links
4. rgb(10, 106, 161) (#0A6AA1): secondary blue
5. rgb(139, 171, 241) (#8BABF1): light periwinkle tiles
6. rgb(230, 48, 138), rgb(245, 118, 0), rgb(0, 191, 125): small category chip pops

Visual pattern worth borrowing (style, not assets): a wide banner made of activity
photos interleaved with solid blue tiles sits on a white page; content below is
white cards with thin borders; one warm cream callout box gives the page its
inviting note. The planner's facelift maps this to: white ground, MDC Blue
banner bands with photos breathing through an overlay, the warm cream kept for
notice boxes, and the validated category hues playing the role of the chip pops.
