Civic Action Scorecard Planner

A free student-built planning tool for iCED's Civic Action Scorecard, 2026-2027 edition.

124actions tracked
300points to Gold
0cost, accounts optional

What it does

  • Four suggested phases pace the year; checking actions tracks points, breadth, and the next award level.
  • A goal line shows the weekly pace to Bronze, Silver, or Gold by any target date.
  • Every action carries its documentation requirement and the What, So What, Now What prompts, with draft boxes that count sentences against each prompt's minimum.
  • "Open EngageMDC" lands on that exact action in the CAS group, one click from Add Impact. Each draft has a copy button matching the form's three boxes.
  • Installs to a phone home screen and works offline. Optional Google sign-in syncs progress across devices.

What it is not

An unofficial planning aid. It submits nothing, stores nothing on MDC systems, and costs iCED nothing. Reflections stay the student's own writing; the planner only carries their drafts to the form. Content is used under iCED's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license with attribution.
